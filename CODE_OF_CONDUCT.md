# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming to people at any level of trading or programming experience.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Show empathy. Trading is a domain where people lose money and feel foolish
  about it; that is not material for jokes at anyone's expense.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- **Using this project to solicit, promote, or sell trading services, signal
  groups, courses, or managed accounts.** This is a tool people run on their own
  machine for their own trading. Issues and discussions are not a marketing
  channel, and anyone arriving to find customers will be removed.

## A note specific to this project

Shani is about someone's money and their own decisions. Two things follow:

**Do not give unsolicited trading advice in issues or reviews.** Discuss the
software. Whether someone should fade the open is not a code review comment.

**Do not overstate what the software can do.** Claiming or implying that this
predicts markets, guarantees results, or constitutes financial advice is a
conduct problem, not just an inaccuracy — it can cost someone real money. The
project is deliberately careful about sample sizes, caveats, and disclaimers,
and contributions are expected to hold that line.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers via a private GitHub security
advisory or direct contact. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, or contributors that violate
this policy, and will explain why when doing so.
