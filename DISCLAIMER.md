# Disclaimer

Read this before running Shani against anything that matters.

## Not financial advice

Shani is software for journaling and analysing your own trading. It is not a
financial advisor, and nothing it produces is investment advice, a
recommendation, or a solicitation to trade.

The agent's "proposals" are **text generated by a language model** from your own
historical trades and public market data. They are a prompt for your own
thinking, not a signal to act on. A model that says a setup has won 4 of 7 times
is reporting your history — it is not predicting the eighth.

## Trading futures involves substantial risk of loss

Futures are leveraged. Losses can exceed your deposit. Most people who trade
them lose money. Past performance — including performance shown by Shani's own
statistics and backtests — does not indicate future results.

Anything derived from a small sample is especially unreliable. A setup card
built from twelve trades is a hypothesis, not an edge.

## Known limitations you are accepting

- **The paper broker is a simulation.** It models slippage and commission, but it
  cannot model queue position, partial fills in thin markets, liquidity gaps, or
  what happens to your order during a genuine fast market. Paper results are
  optimistic. Assume they always are.
- **Plane B depends on an undocumented interface.** It reads TradingView
  Desktop's internal page API through the standard Chromium debug port. That API
  is not a public contract and can change in any TradingView release. When it
  changes, tools fail.
- **Plane A uses an undocumented endpoint** and is subject to rate limiting and
  change without notice.
- **Live broker execution is untested.** It ships disabled, and the code paths
  are unreachable while `allow_live: false`.
- Market data may be delayed, wrong, or missing. Do not rely on Shani as your
  source of truth for prices or positions — reconcile against your broker.
